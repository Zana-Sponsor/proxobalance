## Final spend and automatic refund, lines 2605–2755

Record trusted `completed_at` only after confirmed terminal completion and `refund_eligible_at = completed_at + 2 hours`. Before that time, no unused-budget refund. At/after it, fetch and reconcile sufficiently final provider-authoritative spend; two hours alone does not prove finality. Provider reporting can lag and revise, so unavailable/stale/insufficiently settled spend leaves reconciliation pending with bounded retry while the ad stays completed.

Compute server-side from authoritative stored monetary values: `max(customer paid/reserved media amount - reconciled provider spend, 0)`. The implementation interprets source line 2630's **paid/reserved** budget as the original captured customer debit after discounts; current TikTok checkout has no separate service-fee component. It is distinct from the gross daily-budget × duration allocation used for the 95% threshold. For a $10 allocation with a $2 discount, customer paid media is $8: $7.90 final spend returns $0.10; $9.90 returns zero. A refund cannot exceed the original captured debit. Without a discount, $10/$9.90 yields $0.10; $10/$10 or $10/$10.15 yields zero. Do not include fees as ad budget, guess spend, charge overspend, credit twice, create a separate economic chain or silently reverse a committed refund after later reports. Preserve original debit, linked refund transaction/reference, spent/refunded amounts and reconciliation timestamps. Existing wallet/ledger/history must remain authoritative. Repeated and concurrent workers return the committed result. Rejection/cancellation paths retain their separate existing safety rules.
