# Payment provider marks

Original marks retrieved from each provider’s official website on 2026-10-15; exact URLs and hashes are in SOURCES.json. They identify the payment provider only. They are trademarks of their respective owners, not Solar icons and not licensed as open-source artwork. No recoloring, stretching, clipping or claimed endorsement. Simple CSS declarations are inlined where required by Flutter SVG; geometry and brand colors are retained.

ZainCash integration guidelines: https://docs.zaincash.iq/ (Brand Identity). FastPay now uses the unchanged official `https://fast-pay.iq/img/clogo.png` wordmark, replacing the padded JPEG in the deposit grid. Unconfigured rails are disabled, and the unidentified legacy `SuperApp` placeholder is not presented as a verified provider. No new payment integration or recipient account is configured by this visual update.
