# Payment provider marks

These are original provider marks, not drawn approximations. Exact official source URLs, download/check dates and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `SOURCES.json`. They identify the provider only and remain trademarks of their respective owners. No open-source artwork license, merchant agreement or endorsement is asserted. Simple CSS declarations are inlined where required by Flutter SVG; geometry, aspect ratio and brand colors are retained. Do not tint, stretch, clip, add effects to or combine these marks with another symbol.
